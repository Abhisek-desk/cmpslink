# 7-minute demo

1. Login as placement admin.
2. Open dashboard and show students/readiness/offers/at-risk metrics.
3. Open Students and show readiness scores.
4. Login as recruiter or use recruiter account.
5. Create/open a job.
6. Run AI Match and show ranked candidates + reasons.
7. Open Drives and show conflict detection.
8. Open Offers and show lifecycle tracking.
9. Explain the AI layer:
   Resume parsing → embeddings → hybrid matching → explainability → risk prediction.
10. Finish with scalability: multi-campus data, role-based access, MongoDB, AI microservice.
